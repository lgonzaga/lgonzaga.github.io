  Adjunct Professor
  Unisinos University, Polytechnic School <br>
  Head of
  VIZLab (Visual Computing & GeoInformatics Lab)

As a Professor at Unisinos University in South Brazil, I lead the Vizlab (Center of Excellence Geoinformatics & Visual Computing). My expertise lies in immersive virtual reality, x-reality, and leveraging multi-source data to tackle complex problems. At Vizlab, I've spearheaded projects focusing on technologies for the petroleum geology, significantly advancing the field of immersive virtual geology.

My current focus includes transforming the education and learning experience in geology and other application areas through immersive technologies. This involves innovative approaches to integrate data from multiple remote sensors and laboratory data, primarily using Machine Learning and Deep Learning techniques. This work aims to predict quantitative parameters and extract knowledge, achieving enriched virtual enviroment and disruptive virtual experiences.

I am proficient in coding, primarily in C++ and Python, and skilled in using tools like OpenGL, OpenCV, Unreal Engine, and PyTorch. My work often involves applying these skills to develop advanced solutions in virtual and mixed reality, as well as in stereo 360-degree images jointly with lidar and depth sensors, and sensors in the non-visible spectrum. My passion for these technologies drives me to explore new frontiers in visual computing, significantly contributing to advancements in technologies for hyper immersive reality.
