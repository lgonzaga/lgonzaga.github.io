  Professor
  Unisinos University, Polytechnic School <br>
  Head of
  VIZLab (Center of Excellence Geoinformatics & Visual Computing)

As a Professor at Unisinos University in South Brazil, I lead the Vizlab (Center of Excellence Geoinformatics & Visual Computing). My expertise lies in immersive virtual reality, x-reality, and leveraging multi-source data to tackle complex problems. At Vizlab, I've spearheaded projects focusing on technologies for different application areas, such as the petroleum geology, significantly advancing the field of immersive virtual geology.

My research combines immersive multimodal visualization, multimodal image analysis and applied computer vision, with strong ties to geoinformatics and remote sensing. I work with imaging sensors across the ultraviolet (UV), visible and infrared (VNIR, SWIR, MWIR and LWIR) spectrum, and apply this framework to strategic problems in oil & gas exploration, road safety and rare earth elements in mining. Our profile is to conduct applied research and turn it into innovative solutions for these areas.

My current focus includes transforming the education and learning experience in geology and other application areas through immersive technologies. This involves innovative approaches to integrate data from multiple remote sensors and laboratory data, primarily using image processing, computer vision, machine learning and deep learning techniques. This work aims to predict quantitative parameters and extract knowledge, achieving enriched virtual environment and disruptive virtual experiences.

I have a strong background in programming, especially in C++ and Python, and I'm adept at leveraging technologies such as OpenGL, OpenCV, Unreal Engine, and PyTorch. My expertise is frequently utilized in crafting cutting-edge applications in virtual and mixed reality environments, alongside integration with stereo 360-degree imagery, lidar and depth sensors, as well as sensors operating beyond the visible spectrum. Driven by a deep enthusiasm for these fields, I continuously seek to pioneer new developments in visual computing, playing a key role in the evolution of hyper-immersive reality technologies.
